ShopEase FAQs

How do I place an order? +

Browse products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Enter your details and complete the payment.

Do I need an account to order? +

No, but creating an account helps you track orders and manage your wishlist easily.

How can I track my order? +

You can track your order from the “My Orders” section or via email/SMS updates.

What payment methods are accepted? +

We accept Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery.

How long does delivery take? +

Delivery usually takes 3–7 business days depending on your location.

What is your return policy? +

You can return products within 7 days if they are unused and in original packaging.

How will I get my refund? +

Refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to your original payment method.

Can I cancel my order? +

Yes, you can cancel your order before it is shipped.
